# Development linguistic coverage

This audit classifies the frozen 370 development cases and their 380 actual owner turns. Labels overlap: a direct request can use a paraphrase, contain quoted data and require several tools. Counts describe authored language and objectives, not successful routing, model accuracy or empirical difficulty. No routing, inference, handler execution or test workload was used for this audit.

The corpus remains unchanged at SHA-256 `5ec39bed6713be86a1be4d846df92f6e104bdce6cec8021562ca06c72c5e8a52`. The full per-turn labels and definitions are in [development-linguistic-coverage.json](development-linguistic-coverage.json). Only development queries and their already-frozen oracles were read; final material was not accessed.

| Direct | 362 | An identifiable operation/question and target are stated in the turn. Conversational framing is allowed; missing-target cases and seven context-dependent follow-ups are excluded. The development set has 318 medium, 52 hard and zero easy **cases**. The author's original rule assigns hard to retained-history cases, cases with multiple independently required operation groups, and the quoted-data/mixed-intent, ambiguous-request and denied-action families. The remaining cases are medium; all 50 supplemental contract probes were authored medium. Before the corpus freeze, the original `standard` and `challenging` labels were mapped to `medium` and `hard`. This policy is a coarse author-defined stratification, not a result-based ranking. No labels were changed during this audit. If case labels are inherited by both turns of a sequence, the 380 owner turns contain 318 medium and 62 hard turns.

The set covers 33 authored scenario families, with every registered capability explicitly targeted somewhere and 141 IDs present in required menu groups. These figures describe capability breadth, separate from the linguistic forms above. The small slang, typo, mixed-domain and follow-up counts are coverage limitations. This development corpus is not a balanced language stress set, does not represent broad dialect variation and does not establish robustness from a few successful examples. It contains no authored easy stratum. The audit does not expand the corpus, reclassify its oracles or inspect the independent final set.

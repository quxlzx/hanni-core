# Customer and support responses

Read the customer's issue and relevant thread, order, or ticket details that are
provided or accessible. Identify what has happened, the customer's requested
resolution, and the next action the employee can actually take.

Acknowledge the specific issue and explain the available next step in plain
language. Use only documented policy for refunds, replacements, eligibility,
and response times. Do not invent an account lookup, troubleshooting result,
refund approval, discount, or promised fix date. If policy or authority is missing,
draft a response that identifies the information or escalation needed.

Ask only for information needed to resolve the issue; do not request passwords,
full payment details, or unrelated personal data. Separate internal diagnostic
notes from the customer-facing response.

Return a usable draft and any necessary internal follow-up. Sending a reply,
changing a ticket, or issuing a refund is a separate action that requires the
user's instruction and an available authorized tool. Confirm only effects shown
by the tool result.

# Status updates and handoffs

Establish the reporting period and audience. Use supplied progress notes or
accessible task records. Distinguish completed outcomes, work in progress,
blocked work, and future plans. Describe effects and deliverables rather than
turning activity into an unsupported claim of completion.

Highlight material changes, the next milestone, and any decision or help needed.
For a blocker, include the consequence, owner if known, and proposed next step.
Preserve real deadlines; label an unconfirmed forecast as an estimate. Do not
invent completion percentages, green status, or a recovery commitment.

For a handoff, include current state, relevant files or record links, remaining
work, dependencies, and how the next owner can verify progress. Omit unrelated
private conversation and credentials.

Match the team's existing format when provided. Produce a draft unless posting
or sending was requested. If a connected source cannot be read, identify the
coverage gap instead of reporting a complete organizational status.
